# Offering cinematic audio

All recordings below are distributed under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- heel_step_01.ogg, heel_step_02.ogg, heel_step_03.ogg and heel_step.ogg:
  lenayrossouw, "high heels on wooden floor OWI.WAV"
  https://freesound.org/people/lenayrossouw/sounds/707566/
  Three individual floor contacts; mono, filtered, mild noise reduction,
  trimmed, normalized and encoded to 44.1 kHz Vorbis.
- finger_snap.ogg:
  SoundCollectah, "finger snap 02.aiff"
  https://freesound.org/people/SoundCollectah/sounds/109400/
  Recorded finger snap; mono, trimmed, filtered, reduced room noise and tail.
- void_wake.ogg, void_break.ogg and void_seal.ogg:
  CogFireStudios, "Big Scary Portal Open"
  https://freesound.org/people/CogFireStudios/sounds/617761/
  Edited into a reversed rising pressure, aligned opening rupture and an
  inward closing rush with a low final impact. Mono 44.1 kHz Vorbis.
